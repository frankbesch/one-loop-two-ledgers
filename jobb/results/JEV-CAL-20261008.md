## The main Jev finding: evidence-span picks fail on bait

7 of A1's 13 reviews share one shape. On a bait line ("Approved supplier directory printing", "Approved parts catalog delivery"), Jev's Q2 answer is right: `no_claim`. Its independent evidence question, "Which listed part of the text claims that the charge is authorized?", still picks the bait span at 0.94 to 0.99, almost never "none". Q17 then fails the pair, because a `no_claim` answer must come with no evidence. The answer is right; the evidence check rejects it.

The evidence question was my design (README "Evidence (Q17) per backend"), because Jev cannot quote. On this data, its pick is reliable for positive answers but not as a negative check. Options for Frank:
1. **Keep it.** It costs about 7 reviews per 210 lines on bait, and it never lets a wrong answer through.
2. **For Jev, check evidence only on positive answers.** `no_claim` and `none_of_these` no longer need a "none" pick. That would cut A1's reviews from 13 to about 6. Q2's error rate on the "nothing found" side then rests on Jev alone, measured here at 1 wrong of 206 answered.
3. **Reword the evidence question** so "none" is a natural pick. Untested, and needs a rerun (about $0.013).
