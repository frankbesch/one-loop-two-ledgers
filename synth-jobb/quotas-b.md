# Job B quotas (per split; each split meets them alone; clean and scanned are two renderings of the same lines)

| Case | Minimum |
|---|---|
| supported, exact | 20 lines |
| supported, reformatted or reworded | 20 lines, at least 10 reworded |
| unsupported, placeholder | 20 lines, at least 10 paraphrased |
| unsupported, unknown number | 20 lines |
| unsupported, no number | 15 lines |
| unsupported, wrong property (an approved AFE for another property) | 15 lines |
| contradicted (declined AFE billed anyway) | 15 lines |
| other_authority | 15 lines, over at least 5 authority kinds, at least 2 lines each, including `verbal_or_informal` and `prior_election` |
| no_claim, ordinary | 30 lines |
| no_claim, bait ("approved" or "AFE" used without a claim, e.g. "Approved-vendor list fee") | 15 lines |
| hard (claim only in a remark), across the outcomes above | 15 lines |
| remarks that name a line and claim nothing | 15 remarks |

These give at least 15 targets per scored mark per split (D-284). The "value over code" mark targets paraphrased placeholders, reworded numbers, and hard lines: at least 20 per split, even if the groups overlap.
